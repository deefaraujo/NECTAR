# Licensing and community

NECTAR uses a source-available model, with a free license limited to personal use and non-commercial study. This model must not be presented as equivalent to GPL, AGPL or MIT, which grant other permissions.

The applicable text is `LICENSE`. For professional or business use, use in commercial products, services for clients, hosting or distribution outside the permissions granted there, obtain written authorization from the owner, Déborah Fernandes: https://github.com/deefaraujo.

The code license does not transfer rights over the name or visual identity, over external projects or over third-party components. Independent fonts and dependencies keep their own licenses.

Under this model it is possible to maintain a community, receive suggestions and study the code. Community or financial support channels, when created by the owner, will be announced through the official channels. A donation does not replace a commercial license agreement.

Public code can be viewed and copied: the license defines permissions, but it is not a technical barrier and does not guarantee the absence of unauthorized use. Private projects must stay out of the repository and its history. Before signing licensing agreements or adopting a definitive contribution policy, legal review of the text and of the ownership of the components is recommended.
